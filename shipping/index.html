Shipping and returns

Local pickup

Local pickup in San Diego. Pick a window at checkout and I will have it ready at the studio.

Shipping

Shipping is insured and calculated by weight and box size. Boxed trees ship in a double-walled carton, braced at the base. Branches ship flat.

Arrives unsealed

The box arrives unsealed inside the carton so you smell the cedar first. Inspect the piece before you decide to keep it.

A honey stick

Every order ships with a local honey stick, tied to the beeswax finish on the wood.

Returns

14 days on unused pieces, in the box they came in. Custom and trade orders are final once the sketch is approved.

If it arrives broken

Photograph the carton and the piece before you move anything and email me within 48 hours. I will replace it.
